中文说明

这是由 Chi Tai 本人使用的个人工具，仅用于将影片上传到他自己的 YouTube 频道「泰国今日焦点」。

本工具通过 YouTube Data API，在 Chi Tai 授权后：

  • 使用权限范围 youtube.upload 上传影片到其本人频道；
  • 使用权限范围 youtube.readonly 读取本人频道与影片信息，以核对上传结果。

没有第三方使用者。本应用不对公众提供登录或授权服务，仅供频道所有者本人在其自有机器上运行；不收集第三方资料。

隐私与资料处理说明请见：隐私政策（Privacy Policy）。

联络：经由 YouTube 频道 @thaitodayfocus 联络。

English

This is a personal tool used only by Chi Tai to upload videos to his own YouTube channel 「泰国今日焦点」.

After Chi Tai authorizes access, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to his own channel with the scope youtube.upload;
  • read his own channel and video information with the scope youtube.readonly to verify uploads.

There are no third-party users. This application does not offer sign-in or authorization to the public. It runs only on the channel owner’s own machine and does not collect third-party data.

For how data is handled, see the Privacy Policy.

Contact via YouTube channel @thaitodayfocus.
