中文

1. 谁使用本应用

本应用是由 Chi Tai 为其 YouTube 频道「泰国今日焦点」使用的个人工具。只有频道所有者本人授权与使用。不向第三方用户收集资料，也不向公众提供服务。

2. 访问哪些 Google / YouTube 用户数据

在 Chi Tai 通过 Google OAuth 明确授权后，本应用仅访问其本人 Google 帐号下与 YouTube 相关的数据，使用的权限范围为：

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — 上传影片到本人频道；
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — 读取本人频道与影片信息，以核对上传是否成功。

本应用不会请求 Gmail、Drive、通讯录或其他无关 Google 服务的权限，也不会访问任何第三方用户的数据。

3. 如何使用、存储与共享这些数据

  • 使用：仅用于将本人制作的影片上传到本人 YouTube 频道，并读取上传结果（例如 video ID、处理/隐私状态）以完成核对与排程发布检查。
  • 存储：OAuth 令牌保存在所有者本人机器上的本地凭证目录，并设置严格的文件系统权限；令牌不会上传到第三方服务器，也不会与他人共享。
  • 日志：本地日志仅保留与上传相关的技术标识（例如 video ID、内容哈希），不记录完整访问令牌或无关个人资料。
  • 共享 / 出售：本应用不出售、不出租、不向广告商或分析服务共享 Google 用户数据。没有内嵌广告，也没有第三方分析或追踪脚本。

4. 有限使用（Limited Use）声明

「泰国今日焦点 上传工具」对通过 YouTube Data API 自 Google 取得的用户数据之使用，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其 Limited Use 要求：该等数据仅用于提供或改进面向用户的功能（本应用即：向本人频道上传并核对影片），不会用于其他目的（包括但不限于投放广告、出售给数据经纪商，或与无关第三方共享），也不会以违反该政策的方式传输或披露。

5. 相关条款与政策

  • Google 隐私权政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • YouTube 服务条款：https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

6. 如何撤销授权

Chi Tai 可随时在 Google 帐号权限页面撤销本应用的访问：https://myaccount.google.com/permissions。撤销后，本地保存的令牌将无法再刷新；亦可删除本机凭证文件以停止使用。

7. 变更

若本政策对 Google 用户数据的使用方式有实质变更，将更新本页并调整生效日期。联络：经由 YouTube 频道 @thaitodayfocus。

English

1. Who uses this app

This application is a personal tool used by Chi Tai for his YouTube channel 「泰国今日焦点」. Only the channel owner authorizes and uses it. It does not collect data from third-party users and does not offer a public service.

2. What Google / YouTube user data is accessed

After Chi Tai explicitly authorizes access via Google OAuth, the app accesses only data from his own Google account related to YouTube, using these scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — upload videos to his own channel;
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — read his own channel and video information to verify uploads.

The app does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts, or other unrelated Google services, and does not access any third party’s data.

3. How data is used, stored, and shared

  • Use: solely to upload videos the owner created to the owner’s YouTube channel, and to read upload results (for example video ID and processing/privacy status) for verification and scheduled-publish checks.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner’s machine in a credentials directory with restricted filesystem permissions. Tokens are never uploaded to a third-party server and are never shared with others.
  • Logs: local logs keep only technical identifiers related to uploads (for example video IDs and content hashes). They do not store full access tokens or unrelated personal data.
  • Sharing / selling: the app does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers or analytics services. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking scripts.

4. Limited Use statement

「泰国今日焦点 上传工具」’s use of information received from Google APIs (YouTube Data API) will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: such data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in this app (uploading and verifying videos on the owner’s own channel), is not used for other purposes (including advertising, sale to data brokers, or unrelated third-party sharing), and is not transferred or disclosed in ways that violate that policy.

5. Related policies

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

6. How to revoke access

Chi Tai may revoke this app’s access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, locally stored tokens can no longer be refreshed; the local credential files may also be deleted to stop use.

7. Changes

If this policy materially changes how Google user data is used, this page will be updated and the effective date adjusted. Contact via YouTube channel @thaitodayfocus.
